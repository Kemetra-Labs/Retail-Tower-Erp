**Recommended direction: B's composition with A's token discipline ("B-lite").** This means:

- the Ink band and the PIN dock (OD-1 scoped, OD-6, OD-7);
- the Teal commit (OD-2 rejected);
- Dubai only (OD-3 deferred);
- flat panels (OD-4 rejected);
- ornament confined to the band (OD-5 narrow).

**Why.** It keeps what the owner liked: a strong brand band, large cashier cards, and the name above a keypad that never moves. It does not create a second commit colour or a second panel grammar inside a product whose sale and checkout commits are Teal. B as drawn shows the full Console look, so the owner can judge the difference. B-lite is B with OD-2/3/4 answered as recommended: the Teal commit and flat panels in A's boards show exactly those parts.
