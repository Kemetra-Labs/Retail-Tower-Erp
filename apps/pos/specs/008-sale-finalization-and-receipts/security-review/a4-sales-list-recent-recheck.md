## 4. Findings and their disposition

| Severity | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Disposition                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LOW      | `invalid_input` refusals are not audited.                                                                                                                                                                                                | Accepted: malformed input, no data returned; the same posture as `sales.list` (`invalid_request`).                                                                            |
| LOW      | If the injected clock returned an invalid `Date`, `businessDayOf` would throw and reject the invoke.                                                                                                                                     | Accepted: the production clock is `new Date()`; an invalid clock is a process fault, not input, and the rejection is visible rather than a wrong day.                         |
| INFO     | **Window mechanism differs from doc 20 §3.4 in form, not in meaning.** The doc selects `finalized_at >= now − 48 h` and keeps rows whose Cairo day equals today's. This slice reads the exact half-open range of today's business day instead. | The same rows, with no daylight-saving arithmetic and no JavaScript day filter. `businessDayBounds` is the RT-541 helper and is already covered for the DST days.            |
| INFO     | `sales.readForHandoff` (doc 20 §5, listed under N-S1 in §8) is not in RT-1189's scope or acceptance criteria; it serves Completion (N-S2).                                                                                               | Not built here. When N-S2 adds it, it needs its own §A4 entry.                                                                                                                |
